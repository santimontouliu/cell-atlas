A window into cell biology.

This is an educational, three-dimensional model of a generalized animal cell. Structures, movement, abundance, timing, and energy output are simplified, and sizes are not to scale. Organelle animations illustrate selected mechanisms; they are not molecular dynamics simulations. Real cells are crowded systems with many simultaneous chemical reactions.

The guided journey follows a secreted protein; many other proteins stay in the cytosol or take other routes. Oxygen affects aerobic ATP production, but the slider is illustrative rather than a quantitative biochemical simulation.
